# Doda Screensavers

Doda Screensavers is a collection of 5 cozy, humorous, and highly charming text-mode screensavers. It breaks away from the tired cliches of "Matrix Rain" and "Bouncing Logos," offering delightful little ASCII animations to protect your CRT (or just to make you smile).

## The Screensavers

1. **Terminal Cat:** A little ASCII cat wanders your terminal, occasionally sitting down or falling asleep (with floating `Zzz`s). It might even chase a random bug!
2. **Bored Ghost:** A floating ghost drifts around your screen. Occasionally, it gets bored and slowly types out classic, nostalgic DOS-era computing jokes.
3. **Cozy Campfire:** A warm, crackling ASCII campfire with floating ember particles. Occasionally, a tiny camper walks by to roast a marshmallow.
4. **Clumsy Construction Crew:** Tiny stick figures march on screen to build a block tower. Unfortunately, they are clumsy. The tower will eventually collapse, causing them to throw their hands up in frustration before trying again.
5. **Mischievous Dog:** A stray dog wanders across your screen. Every so often, it will stop to dig a hole or leave a yellow "puddle," kicking up dirt before continuing on its way.

## How to Use

Execute the utility from your terminal to launch the menu:

```bash
python doda_saver.py
```

Use the **Up/Down** arrow keys to select a screensaver and press **ENTER** to start it.
To exit a running screensaver and return to the menu, simply **press any key**.
